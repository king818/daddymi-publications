家人的追思FAMILY TRIBUTES

家人分散在各地——臺灣、美國——每人錄下一段話、寫下一封信。距離再遠，思念一樣抵達。

弟・弟媳三叔 陳翊群 · 小嬸 張淑貞 — 緬懷大哥

大哥，我是陳翊群啊，您還記得嗎？那時我們還在大同初中夜間部就讀，白天總是和您一起開著那台小貨車，奔波於大街小巷，將一箱又一箱的新鮮水果送往各大餐廳。那段青春歲月彷彿才剛發生，一眨眼，竟已走過六十個年頭。

如今，您已走完人生圓滿且快樂的一生。雖然這些回憶是很久以前的事，卻依然深深烙印在我的心中，歷歷在目，溫暖如昨。

在這裡誠心祝福您一路好走，願您在未來的世界裡平安喜樂，從此無病無痛，自在安詳。

永遠懷念您的　翊群淑貞夫婦敬上

ENGLISH VERSION

Dear Brother, this is Chen Yi-Chun. Do you still remember those days when we were studying at Datong Junior High’s night school? During the day, we often drove that little truck together, delivering fresh fruit to restaurants all around the city. In the blink of an eye, sixty years have passed—and now, you’ve completed your joyful and fulfilling journey in this life.

Although those memories are from long ago, they remain vivid in my heart, as if they happened just yesterday.

I sincerely wish you a peaceful journey ahead. May you find peace and joy in the world to come, free from all pain and suffering.

Lovingly and respectfully offered by Yi-Chun and Shu-Zhen

妹・妹婿・外甥女三姑 陳金玉 · 張伯峰 · 張歆昀 — 悼念大哥與大舅

此生有幸成为您的兄妹，我感到无比珍惜。曾经与您一同度过的美好时光，如今回想起来仍历历在目，温暖人心。那些欢笑与陪伴，将永远烙印在我的记忆里。愿您在未来的世界依然幸福安好，一路走好。

It was a blessing in this life to be your sibling, and I truly cherished that bond. The beautiful times we shared are etched in my memory, and they continue to warm my heart. Your laughter and presence will remain with me forever. May you be joyful and at peace in the world beyond. Farewell.

亲爱的大舅，衷心感谢您在我们小时候无微不至的照顾与慷慨。您总是那么体贴地为我准备我最爱的空心菜，几乎天天都为我煮。我还记得那一整个星期，我一再要求吃您亲手煮的香菇面。香气与滋味至今仍让我难忘。

Dear Uncle, thank you from the bottom of my heart for your care and generosity during our childhood. You always thoughtfully cooked my favorite vegetable—water spinach—almost every day just for me. I still remember how I asked you to make your signature mushroom noodles again and again for a whole week. The aroma and taste remain unforgettable.

在加州的日子里，您总带着我们去烤肉、钓鱼，笑声不断。您用满满的爱心，每天准备丰盛的饭菜，特别是那一盘盘新鲜可口的空心菜。那是我们童年最温馨的味道。

During our time in California, you took us out for barbecues and fishing—those were joyful, laughter-filled days. You poured your love into every meal you made, especially the fresh, delicious water spinach we all loved. That flavor became one of the warmest memories of our childhood.

感谢您给予我们的一切——不仅是照顾，更是无私的爱。愿您在天国安详，继续喝着您喜爱的咖啡，微笑地看着我们。我们会永远想念您。一路好走，大舅。

Thank you for everything you gave us—not just your care, but your unconditional love. May you rest peacefully in heaven, sipping your favorite coffee, smiling down on us. You will always be missed. Farewell, dear Uncle.

外甥黃柏翰 Danny · 黃冠傑 Robin — 給親愛的阿公

阿公，太久沒見了，已經十五年了。我今年三十五歲了，現在已經有了三個孩子，有一個兒子王大，還有兩個女兒。本來我們計劃明年要去看你，讓他們也能見到你，讓你看看我們的孩子們。但如今，只能把我們的思念寄託在這封信中。

It’s been so long, Grandpa—15 years since we’ve seen you. I’m 35 now, with three children of my own. We were planning to visit next year, to let the kids meet you, to introduce them to the man I’ve always looked up to.

從小到大，我一直記得你帶我去釣魚，陪我看我打球、打棒球的樣子。後來我長大了，你也見過我的老婆，我還記得你對她說的話，那些溫暖的祝福我一直記得。現在你可以和阿嬤團聚了，我相信你們在一起一定很開心。

I’ll never forget those days you came to America, Grandpa—catching the best fish, cooking those delicious soups, and filling our home with your love. You told me to live my life to the fullest, to take control of my destiny—and I’ve carried that lesson with me every day.

謝謝你曾經為我們所做的一切。你煮飯、帶我們出去玩、為我們付出。我小時候的這些回憶，至今仍深深留在我的心裡。

We’re so grateful you were a part of our lives. Your presence, your laughter, your stories—they shaped who we are.

阿公，我真的很想你。我們全家都很想你，媽媽也很想你。但請你放心，我們一定會照顧好媽媽和爸爸。你在天上和阿嬤一起，不再有病痛，可以安安穩穩地休息、吃你喜歡吃的、看著我們慢慢長大。

Please watch over us from above, Grandpa—with Grandma by your side, smiling down on us. Rest well, eat well, and be joyful.

我們永遠記得你，永遠愛你。

Love you forever，阿公。

孫女Annie Chiang 江蕾 — 追思親愛的阿公

親愛的阿公：

我是 Annie。我很感恩今年回台灣時，有機會能再見到您。我一直記得小時候去您家，您都會為我煮那道我最喜歡的豬腳，還記得我們出去吃飯，您總是點我愛吃的菜，讓我從來沒有餓過肚子。那份被您疼愛的感覺，我會永遠記在心裡。

阿公，一路好走。我相信您現在已經和阿嬤團聚了，我會一直想念您，直到我們有一天再見面。

永遠愛您的，Annie

ENGLISH VERSION

Dear Grandpa, This is Annie. I feel so blessed that I had the chance to see you again when I visited Taiwan this year. I still remember how, when I was little, you always cooked my favorite pig’s feet dish when I came to visit. Whenever we went out to eat, you always ordered the dishes I loved the most. With you, I was never hungry — only filled with love.

Grandpa, rest in peace. Now you and Grandma can finally be together again. I miss you so much, and I’ll always carry you in my heart until we meet again someday. With all my love, Annie

孫女Emily（糯米 Nomi）— 追憶阿公時光

親愛的阿公：

我是糯米（Nomi）。我真的很想你。我記得我們以前一起包水餃的時光，還有當我沒吃飯的時候，你總是會帶我去餐廳，那裡的食物都好好吃。

我也記得我們一起去 Marina 永和超市買魚，然後你會親手煮出美味的料理，像是麵啊、湯啊，真的都很好吃。你還會教我怎麼煮湯，雖然我現在不知道怎麼說那個字，但我還記得那個湯的味道和我們一起做的快樂時光。

你常常帶我出去玩，每天都開車載我去學校。這些回憶我一直記在心裡。阿公，我真的很想你。

永遠愛你的　糯米（Nomi）

ENGLISH VERSION

Dear Agong, This is Nomi. I miss you so much. I remember how we used to make dumplings together, and when I hadn’t eaten yet, you would take me to a restaurant — the food was always so delicious.

I also remember going with you to Marina Yong He Supermarket to buy fish. Then you’d cook amazing meals for me — like noodles and soup. They were so good. You even taught me how to make soup… I don’t know the word for it now, but I still remember how fun it was and how good it tasted.

You always took me out to have fun, and every day you would drive me to school. I keep all these memories in my heart. Agong, I really miss you. With love, Nomi

孫女Sophia 蘇菲 — 阿公的溫柔愛

各位親友大家好，今天，我想和大家分享一些我成長過程中最珍貴的回憶，那些與阿公一起度過的日子，是我一輩子都不會忘記的寶藏。

Hello everyone, Today, I’d like to share some of the most cherished memories from my childhood—precious moments spent with my beloved Agong. These memories are treasures I’ll carry with me forever.

我還記得，幾乎每天放學後，阿公都會帶我去他最喜歡的小吃店。不管是麵條還是水餃，只要我能把餐點吃光光，阿公就會給我一塊錢。他總是笑著說：「吃完就有獎勵喔！」我把那些一塊錢存起來，到了週末，我們會一起去 Walmart 或其他商店買玩具，阿公總是耐心地陪著我選擇我最喜歡的東西。

I still remember how almost every day after school, Agong would take me to his favorite places to eat. Whether it was noodles or dumplings, he’d make a deal with me: “If you finish all your food, I’ll give you a one-dollar bill.” I would save up those one-dollar bills and, on the weekends, he’d take me to Walmart or other stores to pick out my favorite toys. He was always so patient and joyful during those times.

除了吃東西，我和阿公還有一個共同的嗜好——釣魚。幾乎每個週末，我們都會去釣魚。他會教我怎麼拋竿、怎麼耐心等待，風大雨大都澆不熄我們的熱情。這些時光讓我學會了專注與平靜，也讓我感受到阿公無聲的愛。

Another special memory we shared was fishing. Almost every weekend, we’d go out together. Agong taught me how to cast a line and wait patiently. Rain or shine, nothing could stop our little adventures. Through those quiet moments by the water, I learned focus, patience, and most of all, I felt his deep, quiet love.

如今，阿公雖然離開了我們，但他留給我的愛與回憶會永遠陪伴著我。他的笑容、他的鼓勵、還有他那句熟悉的加油，都在我心中活著。

Now that he has left us, I know his love and those beautiful memories will stay with me forever. His smile, his encouragement, and those simple but powerful words—“Jiayou, Jiayou, Jiayou”—live on in my heart.

阿公，謝謝您陪我走過童年，謝謝您用行動教會我什麼是愛。我會非常想念您，也會永遠懷念您。

Thank you, Agong, for walking with me through my childhood. Thank you for showing me what love looks like through your actions. I will miss you deeply, and I will cherish your memory forever.

加油，加油，加油，阿公。

Jiayou, Jiayou, Jiayou, Agong.
