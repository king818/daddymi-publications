How it works

This is a pre-sale. Plainly:

  1. Tell us the story — Who it's for, the one thing the song must say, and three memories only the two of you would recognize. Thin stories make thin songs — so we ask properly, once.
  2. You pay first — then we build — We start creating only after your payment; nothing is made before that. Your refund promise is covered from the moment you pay.
  3. You approve the lyrics — You read and approve the exact words before any music exists, and the pronunciation of every name is checked. Change your mind before you approve: full refund.
  4. We make your song — Several versions are made; a person chooses the best one and checks it against your approved words — the name, the lines, the feeling you asked for.
  5. Preview, then delivery — You hear a preview first. Accept it, settle any balance, and your song arrives in full. Delivery is 5 business days after your story is complete and your lyrics are approved — and the date we confirm with you in writing is the date we keep.
