About Lives of Light

Lives of Light is a character-based motivational series. We tell the roads real people have walked — immigrants, athletes, artists, builders — not to dwell on their past, but to use their stories as fuel for your own road.

The Gospel runs through every episode, with an inner reading of achievement: what is accomplished outwardly begins with identity, faith, and hope inwardly. Our promise is simple — when an episode ends, we want you to feel it: life has hope.

Visuals and narration in this series are AI-assisted; stories and scripts are chosen and reviewed by the Daddy Mi team, and AI involvement is disclosed as a matter of policy.
