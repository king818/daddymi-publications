What this is

Gospel Wisdom is a daily devotional being compiled day by day. Each entry takes one scripture passage, sets out its context in its original sense, then reads it through Neville Goddard's inner-interpretation method, and lands on one practical application and a prayer. The interpretive method is labeled as such; no outcomes are promised.

As of November 8, 2026, 17 entries are complete (Chinese + English manuscripts). The full work targets 365 entries, gathered in volumes of 30; the recent compilation pace is about one entry per day.
