这份索引是什么

Neville Goddard 的书和讲座里引用了大量经文，却很少有人把「哪节经文、在哪本书、哪一章」系统地记下来。这份免费索引就是做这件事：每条记录包含经文、他引用它的作品与位置、引用形式，以及这节经文在原有语境中的本义一句话。

核实标准：每条都对着他作品与讲座的全文核对过，引文用他印出的经文原文（他印出的 Psalm 46:10「I AM」为大写、无逗号）。还没核实的，不收。标「尚未成篇」的条目，是我们接下来写《福音智慧》每日灵修的选题路线图——你点哪条，我们就先考虑哪条。

What this index is

Neville Goddard's books and lectures quote scripture constantly, but almost nobody has systematically recorded which verse appears where. This free index does exactly that: each record gives the verse, the work and place where he cites it, the form of the citation, and one line on what the verse means in its own original context.

Verification standard: every record was checked against the full text of his works and lectures, and quotations follow the scripture wording as printed there (his printed Psalm 46:10 has "I AM" in capitals and no comma). Unverified citations are not listed. Records marked "not yet written" are the roadmap for what our Gospel Wisdom daily devotional writes next — request one, and it moves up the list.

Exodus 3:14

"I AM hath sent me unto you."

Cited in: At Your Command (1939), main text — quoted and made the book's foundation.

Plain sense: at the burning bush, God gives Moses His name — self-existent being itself, before any title or tribe.

Plain sense: a war-time psalm's still point — nations raging, God's people told to stop striving and know who God is.

"Whatsoever things ye desire, when ye pray believe that you have received them, and ye shall have them."

Cited in: Feeling Is the Secret (1944), Chapter 3, "Prayer" — quoted with the reference printed in the text.

Plain sense: Jesus teaches prayer — faith that receives as already given, joined to forgiving others (v. 25).

John 1:1

"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

Cited in: Your Faith Is Your Fortune (1941), Chapter 1, "Before Abraham Was" — one of the chapter's two epigraphs.

Plain sense: John's prologue — before creation, the Word already was: with God, and God.
