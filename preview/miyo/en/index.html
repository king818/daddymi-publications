Mi Yo

米 悠
AI SINGER · AI 歌手

Mi Xi is the morning light; Mi Yo is the light that comes on after dark.
“Yo” means unhurried, far-reaching — a note that lingers. Her songs are not about noise; they are about company:
a lamp left on for everyone still holding on after dark.

ABOUT MI YO

Mi Yo is an AI singer with Daddy Mi, alongside Mi Xi — one sings the morning light, the other sings the night.

Her musical personality is calm and resilient, gentle but never fragile: she does not sing to show off; she sings to keep you company. Her visual world is the city at dusk, a lamp by the window, light moving on night water — the mood of someone who has carried the day to its end and still chooses to light a lamp.

DEBUT SINGLE · SINGLE ALBUM

The God Who Sees Me
EL ROI · 看见我的神
In the wilderness, the water was gone. Hagar held her child and felt unseen by anyone. There, God heard the boy's voice and opened her eyes — she saw a well of water, and she saw hope. This song is for everyone who has ever felt unseen: the place that feels like nowhere is not unseen by Him. From Genesis 21:15–19
