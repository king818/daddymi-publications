Before I See

《未见，仍相信》

米曦 Mi Xi · AI Singer
Mandarin Song · Album Track 8 · PREVIEW

PREVIEW

This is a preview page: anyone with the link can open and play it — no sign-in needed. It has not been officially released yet; the official version will be published separately. This page collects every take of the song for review and comparison.

A song for everyone still walking through the valley who refuses to lose faith. The eyes have not yet seen it, but the heart already holds the vision — we do not have faith because we see hope; we keep hope in the darkness because we have faith. A seed sleeps in the soil: it cannot see spring, yet it carries life. May this song walk with you through the tears of the night, and stand with you again at dawn — I have not yet arrived, but my heart already sees the view.
