MI YO
Rest Beneath the Starlight

Mi Xi is the first light of daybreak,
Mi Yo is the star that shines beneath it.

✦The Light Inside a Name

Inside Mi Xi's name is the character 「曦」 — the first light of daybreak, when the sun rises. Beneath Mi Yo's name hides a star: after the sun goes down, the stars light up quietly — no noise, no hurry — simply keeping company with the people of the night.

「悠」 (yōu) means unhurried ease, and also the lingering note — when a song ends, the light stays. Hers are the songs of the night.

Mi Xi sings the morning; Mi Yo sings the night. Night is the day's time of rest: a heart that has carried its cares all day must, by night, return to a place and settle. Her songs are the ones that let the heart rest.

✦About Mi Yo

Mi Yo is an AI singer with Daddy Mi, a fellow artist of Mi Xi — one sings the morning light, the other the starlight. Calm and gentle in temperament, her voice is first of all companionship: she sings for those who carry the night alone. (Mi Yo is an AI singer; this page is an internal preview.)
