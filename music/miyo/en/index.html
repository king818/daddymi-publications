✦About Mi Yo

Mi Yo is the second music artist to join Gospel Wisdom Multimedia Studio and a new member of the musical family led by Mi Xi. Like starlight in the stillness of night, her music offers a gentle refuge for the heart. Her musical journey begins with two songs: “Rest Beneath the Stars” in English and “별빛이 남은 밤” in Korean.
