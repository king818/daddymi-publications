PREVIEW

This is the single-song preview page for “Starlit Night”: anyone with the link can open and play it — no sign-in needed. The song has not been officially released yet; the official version will be published separately. This page carries Take 1 for review and comparison.

She has spent the day's strength, and her heart has forgotten how to rest. This song sings of a night where the starlight remains: the night is still here — and so is the light. By the window, between the city lights and the stars, she slowly learns to rest again — not waiting until everything is fixed, but resting first, and entrusting the rest to the night and the light.

Round 1's within-family tweaks were judged “still sounds like Mi Xi.” This round uses entirely different singer archetypes: the same lyric snippet under identical conditions, four 48-second clips side by side — Mi Xi's reference first, then three new candidates. These are test clips only, not finished songs; Mi Yo's official voice has not changed and awaits this listening review.

Candidate A · Weathered Folk New-voice test48 sec

A weathered contralto in her late fifties: clearly lower and darker register, dry grainy rasp, almost no vibrato, storyteller delivery close to speech; a single steel-string guitar — no piano-and-strings ballad world.

Candidate B · Midnight Jazz New-voice test48 sec

A smoky, breathy alto with behind-the-beat jazz phrasing and loose rubato; upright bass, brushed drums and electric piano in a 3 a.m. club mood — breath and husk Mi Xi does not have. Long intro; voice enters around 0:28.

Candidate C · Ethereal Soprano New-voice test48 sec

Maximum contrast in the other direction: a light, airy head-voice soprano, youthful and floating; harp arpeggios, soft synth pads and a distant music box. Voice enters around 0:22.

Production QC: all three candidates passed speech-recognition checks — lyric lines complete and in order, diction intelligible, no structural defects; later voice entries are pacing traits of these conditions. Status: awaiting the listening ruling — official voice DNA unchanged, the two songs not regenerated.
