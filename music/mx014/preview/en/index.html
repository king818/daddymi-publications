The Peace You Left Me

《留在心里的平安》

PREVIEW

This is a preview page: anyone with the link can open and play it — no sign-in needed. It has not been officially released yet; the official version will be published separately. This page collects every take of the song for review and comparison.

Peace is not a gift we receive only when the world quiets down; it is the treasure Jesus has already left in our hearts. A single lamp runs through this song: guard the peace He left you, like a light kept burning in the night. The world may be loud, but my heart does not have to wander with it. For everyone worn down by life and tangled in worry — you do not have to be strong right away; only remember: the peace He left is still here.
