Cantonese Song · Official Release · 2026-11-17

The Story Behind the Song

This song tells of a man building his life in Shenzhen: cheated, misled, put through a great deal — yet he never gives up, and step by step finds his way back to the road in his heart. Moved by that perseverance, she prays with him every day; they hold each other up and walk toward the same far horizon — waiting out the storm till daybreak, to build their home upon the rock. May this song walk a stretch of the road with you, too, wherever you are on yours.

Making-of Notes

The story begins in Shenzhen's night rain: a man who has taken wrong turns and been hurt, yet never gives up, walking step by step back toward the direction in his heart.

What she does is keep watch — keeping a lamp lit, folding her hands with his in prayer, waiting for the rain to turn to warm sun. Waiting till daybreak is not waiting for him to conquer the world; it is waiting for him to find his direction and remember the way home — then the two of them set out together, to build a home on the rock.
