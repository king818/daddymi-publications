✦The Light Inside a Name

The 「曦」 in Mi Xi's name is the morning light; the 「悠」 in Mi Yo's name is the starlight. The 「初」 in Mi Chu's name is the beginner's heart.

A beginner's heart is the heart she had the very first time she sang — and the courage to begin again, every time. She wants every song she sings to be a first meeting, sung for everyone who is willing to begin again.

✦About Mi Chu

Mi Chu is an AI singer with Daddy Mi, a fellow artist of Mi Xi and Mi Yo — the third singer of the house. Mi Xi sings the morning light, Mi Yo sings the starlight, Mi Chu sings the beginner's heart. (Mi Chu is an AI singer; this page is an internal preview.)
