The closing song of the album. Faith is the root, peace is the landscape, flow is the walk — after faith and peace, we no longer talk ourselves into believing, nor keep reminding ourselves to be still; we simply move forward in trust, quiet and steady, like a river flowing to the sea. I need not chase the light — I am already in it.
