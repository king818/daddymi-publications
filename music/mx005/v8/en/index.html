Making-of Notes

This song was written for a mother who is ill. Her story is true: she loved her family with a lifetime of meals — three hundred sixty-five days a year, from the morning stove to the abundant dinner table every night, without a break; in that bowl of lei cha she ground with her own hands, peanuts and sesame were slowly ground fragrant; only after everyone at the full table had finished did she sit down, last of all, to eat.

Now she is ill. Her story has been told again and again, until the one telling it is the first to tear up — the story came first; then came this song.

First, what this page is: these three takes (seeds 808 / 909 / 1010) were generated in the early hours of 2026-11-11 from intermediate inputs — the lyrics and style files were still unfinished intermediate versions, not the final frozen ones — and their filenames collided with another batch. They were set aside as mislabeled drafts: never presented, never QC-checked.

They have had no QC at all: no line-by-line alignment, no opening-gate test, and what is sung may not match the final lyrics exactly; the lyrics printed below are the final frozen V9.4 sheet, for reference only. At the boss's own request, the three drafts are published here as-is on 2026-11-11 for his listening — they are not final-version candidates.
