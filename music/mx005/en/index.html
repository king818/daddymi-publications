Making-of Notes

This song was written for a mother who is ill. Her story is true: she loved her family with a lifetime of meals — three hundred sixty-five days a year, from the morning stove to the abundant dinner table every night, without a break; in that bowl of lei cha she ground with her own hands, peanuts and sesame were slowly ground fragrant; only after everyone at the full table had finished did she sit down, last of all, to eat.

Now she is ill. Her story has been told again and again, until the one telling it is the first to tear up — the story came first; then came this song.

The song opens half-spoken, half-sung — like reading a qiaopi letter aloud — slowly read into song; and at the end it returns to a soft spoken voice, to finish saying what it has to say. The piano stays close against the voice, like company beneath the lamp. Officially released on 2026-11-11.

The ground note of this song is a kitchen full of wood smoke, long ago back home: before the age of fourteen, the family cooked on an old-style stove burning wood and coal — the heat and white smoke rising from the stove in these lyrics come from a kitchen like that. Later the family moved to Tianhe, Guangzhou, and the kitchen changed; but the meals their mother cooked on that stove never changed, not for a single day.
